🛡️ Shield Design Proposals

Six directions for the screen users see when they open a blocked app. Pick one (or mix elements) and I'll build it.

Reality check: iOS renders the shield from a fixed template — background, one icon image, title, subtitle, two buttons. The motion below shows the vibe; on device the icon ships as a richly-rendered static image. The big differentiator is what we draw into that icon (and dynamic data like your streak or a live vocab word).
